Where to look — pages and states

Open the pages locally from main and walk them in both themes and at 390 px. The app has no runtime theme toggle — by design: dark activates via the OS preference (prefers-color-scheme) or by stamping data-theme="dark" on <html>. All 7 pages keep scrollWidth == clientWidth == 390 at 390 px.

  • Overview — docs/lab/site/index.html — freshness banner (age ticks every second, honest <noscript>), question-type chips in semantic color, meter warnings in status paint.
  • Fleet — docs/lab/site/fleet.html — the reconciliation bridge above the cards («silent is not dead»).
  • Tasks — docs/lab/site/tasks.html — full detail values wrap instead of the 90-char cut; single PR links; epic chips distinguish ok/warn.

Debatable decisions — worth your eyes

Honest list of the places where the implementation chose a side. None of them is hidden in the code; each is deliberate and each is reversible if you disagree.

  1. Tabs, not a sidebar. The spec (decision q-2026-07-20-008) picked horizontal wrapping tabs over a Mastra-style sidebar — cheaper on mobile, but it is the single biggest deviation from the visual reference. Confirm the navigation feel is what you wanted.
  2. Table cells cap at 34ch with break-word, not anywhere. theme.ts:263 (td{overflow-wrap:break-word;max-width:34ch}); the comment at theme.ts:248 calls the break-word-vs-anywhere difference load-bearing. Long unbroken tokens fold later than with anywhere; check Tasks/Timeline tables with long paths.
  3. Status colors scoped to span (GSL-124). A bare .ok would flood a whole container (the GSL-119 italic-leak lesson). Cost of the choice: a future status element that is not a <span> silently loses its paint.
  4. Traffic-light CVD limitation. The green/amber/red status triple cannot pass the dataviz categorical ΔE gate under deuteranopia (red↔green ΔE 4.2) — by construction, inherited from GSL-109. Mitigation: status is never carried by color alone — a word sits next to the color, the banner scales by shape, numbers are not painted with status color. Accepted consciously; flagged so it is your decision, not ours.
  5. Screenshot folder discrepancy (finalizer's finding). Ticket GSL-138 said assets/GSL-111/; the artifacts actually live per-task in docs/dev_history/assets/GSL-138/ per the orchestrator's live brief. The copies in this report's assets/ are the same files.
